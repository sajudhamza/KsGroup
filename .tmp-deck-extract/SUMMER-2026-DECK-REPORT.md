### The KS Standard — 10 principles (Slide 4) — NEW site-worthy content
1. **Purpose Before Concept** — Every venue needs a clear reason to exist before it needs a logo, menu, or opening party.
2. **Hospitality Before Ego** — The room is built to make guests feel seen, welcomed, and genuinely taken care of.
3. **Discipline Before Scale** — Growth only works when the systems, standards, and people are strong enough to support it.
4. **Energy Before Decoration** — A beautiful room means nothing if it does not have warmth, rhythm, and life.
5. **Details Are the Brand** — Lighting, music, menu language, service, and goodbye all carry the story.
6. **People Make the Place** — A venue becomes valuable when the team believes in it, protects it, and delivers it consistently.
7. **Slow Nights Need Strategy. Busy Nights Need Systems.** — Build for both momentum and control.
8. **The Best Hospitality Feels Effortless** — Because the hard work is invisible.
9. **Culture is Not Posted** — It is programmed, partnered, and repeated.
10. **Build for the Return** — The goal is not one great night, but lasting relevance and repeat business.

### F&B / Events framing (Slides 8–9)
- **F&B:** “Hospitality remembered through ritual.” Menus, cocktails, service rhythms, music, events, uniforms, signage, seasonal programming.
- Pillars: Chef-Driven Menus · Operationally Focused · Beverage Expertise · Quality Sourcing
- **Events:** Private events as “repeatable growth channel” — Branded Events, Weddings & Buyouts, Influencer Activations, Holidays, Corporate Events, Live Programming

### Audience / network (Slide 7)
